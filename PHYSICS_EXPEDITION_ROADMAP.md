# ISC Class XI Physics Expedition Campaign Roadmap
## 🌌 Master Design Blueprint (v0.3.1)

> "If a student remembers the feeling of discovering a law of nature before remembering the formula itself, we have succeeded."

This document establishes the flagship architectural and pedagogical blueprint for the **ISC Class XI Physics Expedition Campaign**. Rather than presenting a series of disconnected virtual laboratories, this campaign is structured as a continuous, singular, and emotionally resonant journey of scientific discovery across deep space and time.

## 🪐 Detailed Expedition Design Sheets

### 01. Expedition Name: Vector Horizons (Kinematics)
*   **Core Scientific Concept:** Motion in a plane, vector deconstruction, independent horizontal & vertical velocities, parabolic trajectories under constant gravity.
*   **Story Premise:** An emergency supply crate must be dropped from a high altitude electromagnetic rail-cannon onto a research outpost nestled deep inside a Martian crater. If the speed is miscalculated, the cargo slams into the crater mountain walls; if dropped correctly, the orthogonal vectors weave a perfect parabola.
*   **World / Environment:** Tharsis Ridge, Mars—a cold, barren red landscape lit by a setting amber sun, surrounded by glowing plasma shielding grids and massive impact craters.
*   **Natural Interaction Language:** Target-calibrated railgun launcher with real-time vector trails. Adjustable sliders for elevation angle ($\theta$), muzzle velocity ($v_0$), and gravitational strength ($g$).
*   **Prediction:** *"Drop an orange flag where you predict the cargo crate will land given its launch elevation and speed on Mars."*
*   **Experiment:** Active launch sequence with a dynamic path trace, displaying force, velocity, and gravity vectors. Enforces a vacuum drop to first isolate pure kinematic equations.
*   **Reflection:** Side-by-side comparison of the landing coordinates and the prediction flag, prompting students to calculate the kinematic delta margin and resolve the independence of horizontal speed from gravity.
*   **Common Misconceptions:** 
    *   *MISCONCEPTION_MASS_DEPENDENT_GRAVITY*: Believing heavier cargo safes fall faster. (Triggered by selecting the 500kg Safe vs the 10kg Crate, which triggers a synchronized dual-mass comparative flight path proving identical fall acceleration).
    *   *MISCONCEPTION_VELOCITY_COUPLED_GRAVITY*: Believing faster sideways movement increases downwards pull.
*   **Core Memory:** The breathtaking realization that horizontal motion remains completely unaffected by gravity, allowing a heavy metal box and a feather-light cargo box to trace the exact same curved path in Martian thin air.
*   **Emotional Arc:** High-tension survival briefing $\rightarrow$ Intellectual focus and trajectory calibration $\rightarrow$ Exhilarating relief as the crate lands safely on target.
*   **Scientific Discovery:** *Galileo’s Conic Split*—unlocked when the horizontal velocity is proven to be uniform while vertical velocity accelerates at a constant $3.72 \text{ m/s}^2$.
*   **Teacher Notes:** Ideal for demonstrating that equations of motion apply independently to horizontal ($x$) and vertical ($y$) axes.
*   **Estimated Duration:** 20 Minutes.
*   **Dependencies on previous Expeditions:** None (Flagship Entrypoint).

### 02. Expedition Name: Inertia Bounds (Laws of Motion)
*   **Core Scientific Concept:** Newton's Laws of Motion, static & kinetic friction coefficients, normal force, tension, and non-inertial frames of reference.
*   **Story Premise:** A crew-carrying cargo rover is stranded on an icy slope of Europa. To pull the rover back up to the base camp, researchers must calibrate a tether system, balancing cable tension, surface friction, and the slope's inclination. If tension exceeds limits, the cable snaps; if friction is underestimated, the rover slips into the crevasse.
*   **World / Environment:** The fractured ice plains of Europa—shining neon blue fissures reflecting Jupiter’s giant, swirling orange eye in the black sky.
*   **Natural Interaction Language:** A variable-slope friction slide ramp with real-time force balance diagrams ($F_N, F_f, F_g, T$). Adjustable sliders for mass ($m$), incline angle ($\theta$), static friction coefficient ($\mu_s$), and kinetic friction coefficient ($\mu_k$).
*   **Prediction:** *"Estimate the exact tipping-point incline angle where static friction breaks and kinetic sliding begins for a titanium hull on Europa's ice."*
*   **Experiment:** Slow tilting of the ice deck, tracking the static friction threshold in real-time, followed by a dynamic slide phase when the coefficient slips from static to kinetic.
*   **Reflection:** Interactive Socratic dialogue asking the student to explain why pushing an object to start moving requires more effort than keeping it in motion.
*   **Common Misconceptions:**
    *   *MISCONCEPTION_FRICTION_MASS_INDEPENDENCE*: Believing friction force is purely dependent on texture, ignoring the normal weight component.
    *   *MISCONCEPTION_NORMAL_EQUAL_GRAVITY*: Believing normal force is always equal to $m \cdot g$, even on a steep incline.
*   **Core Memory:** Pushing the tilt angle just past the limit, seeing the exact frame where static friction snaps into kinetic sliding, and Jupiter’s reflection sliding by as the rover moves.
*   **Emotional Arc:** Anxiety of a stranded vehicle $\rightarrow$ Scientific tension of the tilting slope $\rightarrow$ Satisfying pull to safety.
*   **Scientific Discovery:** *Newton's Friction Limit*—the direct mathematical ratio between the normal force and static friction threshold.
*   **Teacher Notes:** Use to teach inclined plane vector resolution: $F_g \sin(\theta)$ vs. $\mu \cdot F_g \cos(\theta)$.
*   **Estimated Duration:** 25 Minutes.
*   **Dependencies on previous Expeditions:** Expedition 01 (requires understanding vector resolution).

### 03. Expedition Name: Energy Depths (Work, Energy & Power)
*   **Core Scientific Concept:** The Work-Energy Theorem, conservation of mechanical energy, elastic vs inelastic collisions, and spring potential.
*   **Story Premise:** An orbital elevator's braking system has suffered a catastrophic failure. A descending escape capsule must engage an emergency electromagnetic spring-dampener at the base of the shaft. To absorb the capsule's kinetic energy without crushing the passengers under lethal G-forces, you must tune the spring constant and compression limits.
*   **World / Environment:** The subterranean core of an oceanic research elevator in the deep Mariana Trench—dimly lit by biological neon glow-worms and humming coolant tubes.
*   **Natural Interaction Language:** A vertical elevator shaft drop simulator. Sliders control drop height ($h$), capsule mass ($m$), and spring constant ($k$).
*   **Prediction:** *"Predict the maximum compression distance of the spring required to stop a 2000kg capsule falling from 50 meters, and estimate if the resulting deceleration remains within safe human limits."*
*   **Experiment:** Release the capsule. A dual-bar real-time energy visualizer tracks the transformation of Gravitational Potential Energy ($U_g$) to Kinetic Energy ($K$) and ultimately into Elastic Potential Energy ($U_e$).
*   **Reflection:** Analyzing the energy curves, identifying the exact point of maximum kinetic energy (equilibrium point), and calculating the total energy conservation score.
*   **Common Misconceptions:**
    *   *MISCONCEPTION_SPRING_CONSTANT_LINEAR_ENERGY*: Believing double compression only requires double energy (ignoring the quadratic $1/2 \cdot k \cdot x^2$ relationship).
    *   *MISCONCEPTION_WORK_INDEPENDENT_PATH*: Believing non-conservative friction work is path-independent.
*   **Core Memory:** The capsule screaming down the shaft, hitting the buffer, and bouncing to a gentle, perfectly balanced stop as the potential energy bars dance in absolute symmetry.
*   **Emotional Arc:** Terror of freefall $\rightarrow$ Technical calibration of safety systems $\rightarrow$ Serene, weightless equilibrium.
*   **Scientific Discovery:** *The Conservative Conservation*—discovering that total mechanical energy remains pristine, simply shifting forms from height to speed to spring compression.
*   **Teacher Notes:** Demonstrates how work done by a conservative force is stored as potential energy, whereas non-conservative forces dissipate it as heat.
*   **Estimated Duration:** 25 Minutes.
*   **Dependencies on previous Expeditions:** Expedition 02 (requires force balance awareness).

### 04. Expedition Name: Kepler's Gravity (Gravitation)
*   **Core Scientific Concept:** Kepler's Laws of Planetary Motion, Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation, orbital speed, escape velocity, and gravitational potential fields.
*   **Story Premise:** A mapping probe needs to enter a stable orbit around a mysterious, highly dense neutron star remnant. If its speed is too slow, it falls into the crushing gravitational well; if too fast, it escapes the solar system forever. You must find the precise orbital speed vectors for circular and elliptical paths.
*   **World / Environment:** The edge of a dying star system—glowing accretion disks, violet gravitational lensing warps, and the silent, deep black expanse of deep space.
*   **Natural Interaction Language:** Orbit injection engine. Adjust orbital distance ($r$), tangential injection velocity ($v_t$), and probe mass ($m_p$) using high-fidelity radial slider rings.
*   **Prediction:** *"Predict the precise tangential speed required to hold a circular orbit around a star of mass M at a distance of 10,000 kilometers, without spiraling in."*
*   **Experiment:** Launch the probe. Watch its orbital path trace. Speed up or slow down time to observe Kepler's second law (sweeping out equal areas in equal intervals of time).
*   **Reflection:** Calculate the orbital eccentricity and verify Kepler's Third Law ($T^2 \propto r^3$) using real empirical run clocks.
*   **Common Misconceptions:**
    *   *MISCONCEPTION_MASS_DEPENDENT_ORBIT*: Believing heavier satellites need to fly faster to stay in orbit (forgetting that orbital velocity $v = \sqrt{G M/r}$ is independent of satellite mass).
    *   *MISCONCEPTION_ZERO_GRAVITY_IN_ORBIT*: Believing astronauts float because there is zero gravity in space, rather than because they are in continuous freefall.
*   **Core Memory:** Seeing the probe swing rapidly around the periapsis of the star, throwing off a trail of purple sparks as it sweeps equal areas of space in perfect harmony.
*   **Emotional Arc:** Overwhelming awe of cosmic scales $\rightarrow$ Intense math calibration $\rightarrow$ Majestic, perpetual orbital flight.
*   **Scientific Discovery:** *Kepler's Harmonious Ratio*—proving that the orbital square of time is perfectly locked to the cube of the orbital radius.
*   **Teacher Notes:** Excellent for showing why satellites do not need propulsion to remain in orbit once velocity is established.
*   **Estimated Duration:** 30 Minutes.
*   **Dependencies on previous Expeditions:** Expedition 01 (parabolic curves and accelerated falls are generalized into orbit trajectories).

### 05. Expedition Name: Torque & Spin (Rotational Kinematics & Rigid Bodies)
*   **Core Scientific Concept:** Angular velocity, torque, moment of inertia, angular momentum conservation, and the rolling-without-slipping condition on inclines.
*   **Story Premise:** An automated drone-wheel must traverse a steep mountain pass. To survive, the drone can dynamically shift its internal mass distribution: expanding weights outwards to increase inertia, or pulling them inwards to increase spin speed. You must guide the drone wheel through high-friction slopes.
*   **World / Environment:** A high-altitude planetary wind-turbine field—massive, spinning carbon-fiber blades piercing through thick, swirling emerald-colored storms.
*   **Natural Interaction Language:** A rolling disk simulator on a ramp. Control internal mass distribution (radius of gyration $k$), ramp angle ($\theta$), and initial push torque ($\tau$).
*   **Prediction:** *"If a solid copper cylinder and a hollow copper pipe of identical mass are released down the same ramp, which will reach the bottom first?"*
*   **Experiment:** Release the two shapes. Watch them roll side-by-side. A graphic overlay shows rotational energy splitting ($K_{rot}$ vs $K_{trans}$).
*   **Reflection:** Students deconstruct how moment of inertia resists rotation, calculating the exact acceleration differences for various shapes (hoop, sphere, cylinder).
*   **Common Misconceptions:**
    *   *MISCONCEPTION_INERTIA_IS_JUST_MASS*: Believing two objects of equal mass always rotate and accelerate identically, ignoring where that mass is located relative to the axis.
    *   *MISCONCEPTION_TORQUE_WITHOUT_ARM*: Believing torque is solely dependent on force, neglecting the pivot arm length.
*   **Core Memory:** The solid cylinder beating the hollow ring in slow-motion, revealing how the ring's outward mass "hoards" kinetic energy into silent spin instead of forward sprint.
*   **Emotional Arc:** Confusion over identical masses $\rightarrow$ Mechanical adjustments $\rightarrow$ Beautiful, synchronized racing physics.
*   **Scientific Discovery:** *The Gyroscope Pivot*—finding that mass distribution is as vital to rotational flight as mass itself is to straight-line flight.
*   **Teacher Notes:** Demystifies the concept of "Radius of Gyration" and the conservation of angular momentum.
*   **Estimated Duration:** 25 Minutes.
*   **Dependencies on previous Expeditions:** Expedition 02 & 03 (combines friction sliding and energy conservation).

### 06. Expedition Name: Stress & Strain (Properties of Bulk Matter)
*   **Core Scientific Concept:** Hooke’s Law, Young’s Modulus, shear modulus, elastic limit, and stress-strain curves.
*   **Story Premise:** A towering suspension bridge spanning a volcanic gorge is undergoing tectonic stress. You must select and strain-test different material alloys under simulated weight loads. If you exceed the elastic limit, the cables undergo plastic deformation and fail.
*   **World / Environment:** The Obsidian Gorge—a jagged dark glass valley with rivers of glowing orange lava flowing below, framed by massive industrial steel anchors.
*   **Natural Interaction Language:** Tensile stress tester. Adjust wire length ($L$), diameter ($d$), applied force ($F$), and material alloy type (Carbon steel, Titanium, Graphene).
*   **Prediction:** *"Estimate the maximum weight limit before a 2mm carbon-fiber cable enters its plastic deformation zone and permanently stretches."*
*   **Experiment:** Slowly ramp up the bridge load. The stress-strain graph draws live, shifting color from green (elastic) to yellow (plastic deformation) to flashing red (fracture point).
*   **Reflection:** Identify the proportional limit and calculate Young’s Modulus from the slope of the green elastic line.
*   **Common Misconceptions:**
    *   *MISCONCEPTION_STRENGTH_IS_ELASTICITY*: Confusing ultimate strength (resisting breaks) with elasticity (how much it stretches and recovers).
    *   *MISCONCEPTION_DIMENSION_INDEPENDENT_STRESS*: Believing stress is just force, disregarding cable cross-sectional area.
*   **Core Memory:** The sudden, ominous high-frequency hum as the cable slips past its elastic limit, changing color and permanently sagging under the strain.
*   **Emotional Arc:** Industrial weight tension $\rightarrow$ Microscopic molecular analysis $\rightarrow$ Deep appreciation for material limits.
*   **Scientific Discovery:** *Hooke's Molecular Spring*—discovering that solid metals are simply massive networks of atomic springs obeying $F = -k \cdot x$.
*   **Teacher Notes:** Helps bridge the gap between microscopic atomic bonds and macroscopic engineering failures.
*   **Estimated Duration:** 20 Minutes.
*   **Dependencies on previous Expeditions:** Expedition 03 (requires spring potential understanding).

### 07. Expedition Name: Fluid Currents (Fluid Mechanics)
*   **Core Scientific Concept:** Pressure variations with depth, Pascal's Law, Archimedes' Principle, viscosity, streamline flow, and Bernoulli's Principle.
*   **Story Premise:** A deep-sea geothermal valve is leaking. You must guide a miniature repair sub through a narrow, variable-width vent pipe. By adjusting the flow speed and tracking pressure drops, you must prevent the sub from being crushed or pulled into the volcanic exhaust.
*   **World / Environment:** The Abyssal Trench Hydrothermal Vents—pitch-black seawater illuminated solely by roaring white-smoker geysers and glowing sub headlights.
*   **Natural Interaction Language:** Venturi tube wind/water tunnel simulator. Adjustable sliders for inlet velocity ($v$), fluid density ($\rho$), and pipe constriction diameter ($D$).
*   **Prediction:** *"Where will the fluid pressure be lowest: in the wide open pipe section, or in the narrow, constricted bottleneck?"*
*   **Experiment:** Pump the water. Tiny glowing flow indicator particles accelerate through the throat. Pressure dials show a sudden, dramatic drop at the bottleneck.
*   **Reflection:** Resolve the classic hydrodynamic paradox: why high-speed fluid has lower pressure than slow-moving fluid (conservation of energy along a streamline).
*   **Common Misconceptions:**
    *   *MISCONCEPTION_COMPRESSION_INCREASES_PRESSURE*: Believing that narrowing a pipe "squeezes" the fluid and increases its static pressure (confusing speed with pressure).
    *   *MISCONCEPTION_BUOYANCY_MASS_DEPENDENT*: Believing heavy objects displace more water than lighter objects of identical volume.
*   **Core Memory:** Watching the sub accelerate like a rocket through the narrow neck while the pressure gauge plunges into a vacuum, illustrating Bernoulli's magic.
*   **Emotional Arc:** Claustrophobic dark navigation $\rightarrow$ Epiphany on speed and pressure $\rightarrow$ Smooth exit into stable waters.
*   **Scientific Discovery:** *The Venturi Paradox*—proving that kinetic energy increases at the direct expense of static pressure when fluid is forced to speed up.
*   **Teacher Notes:** Essential for explaining flight lift, sprayers, and cardiovascular blood flow anomalies.
*   **Estimated Duration:** 25 Minutes.
*   **Dependencies on previous Expeditions:** Expedition 03 (Bernoulli's equation is a restatement of Conservation of Mechanical Energy).

### 08. Expedition Name: Thermal Engines (Thermodynamics)
*   **Core Scientific Concept:** First & Second Laws of Thermodynamics, isothermal vs adiabatic processes, cyclic operations, and Carnot efficiency.
*   **Story Premise:** A geothermal power core on a volcanic island is destabilizing. To cool the core and extract maximum work, you must tune a thermodynamic gas cycle, balancing heat addition, expansion work, and waste heat rejection. If you push compression too fast, the system overheats.
*   **World / Environment:** The Volcanic Caldera of Mauna Loa—rattling earth plates, glowing magma rivers, and a clean, high-tech geothermal research facility built directly into the basalt rock.
*   **Natural Interaction Language:** A PV-diagram (Pressure-Volume) engine control loop. Sliders adjust cylinder compression ratio ($V_{min}/V_{max}$), source heat temperature ($T_H$), and sink cooling temperature ($T_C$).
*   **Prediction:** *"Can we design a perfect thermodynamic heat engine that converts 100% of absorbed geothermal heat into useful electrical work?"*
*   **Experiment:** Run the piston cycle. Watch the working gas dot trace a closed loop on the PV grid (Isotherm $\rightarrow$ Adiabat $\rightarrow$ Isotherm $\rightarrow$ Adiabat), calculating the enclosed work area.
*   **Reflection:** Calculate the absolute Carnot efficiency limit: $\eta = 1 - T_C/T_H$ and explain why waste heat rejection is an inescapable law of the universe.
*   **Common Misconceptions:**
    *   *MISCONCEPTION_100_PERCENT_EFFICIENCY*: Believing that friction-free parts are sufficient to make an engine 100% efficient (ignoring Kelvin-Planck's second law constraint).
    *   *MISCONCEPTION_ADIABATIC_IS_ISOTHERMAL*: Confusing an adiabatic process (no heat exchange) with an isothermal process (constant temperature).
*   **Core Memory:** The realization that even in a frictionless, ideal universe, heat requires a colder place to go, leaving a tax of warm chaos in its wake.
*   **Emotional Arc:** High-danger thermal warning $\rightarrow$ Patient mechanical balancing of cycles $\rightarrow$ Peaceful, stable energy hum.
*   **Scientific Discovery:** *Carnot's Unreachable Star*—the discovery that heat's conversion to work is fundamentally limited by the absolute temperature ratios of the source and sink.
*   **Teacher Notes:** Perfect for visual-graphic understanding of PV work loops and entropy boundaries.
*   **Estimated Duration:** 25 Minutes.
*   **Dependencies on previous Expeditions:** Expedition 03 (work done by gas expansion).

### 09. Expedition Name: Kinetic Swarms (Behavior of Gases)
*   **Core Scientific Concept:** Kinetic Theory of Gases, root-mean-square speed ($v_{rms}$), pressure-temperature relation, and mean free path.
*   **Story Premise:** A leak has occurred in a life-support gas tank on a space cruiser. You must identify the gas composition (Helium, Nitrogen, or Carbon Dioxide) based on the molecular speed distributions of escape particles, adjusting the temperature to stabilize tank internal pressure.
*   **World / Environment:** The Life Support Engineering Deck—sterile white walls, warning lights, cold steam venting from burst valves, and holograms of microscopic atomic collisions.
*   **Natural Interaction Language:** A microscopic box containing bouncing atom-spheres. Sliders adjust container temperature ($T$), container volume ($V$), and atomic molar mass ($M$).
*   **Prediction:** *"If you heat a container of mixed light Helium atoms and heavy Carbon Dioxide molecules, which atoms will have the higher average kinetic energy? Which will move faster?"*
*   **Experiment:** Raise the temperature. Watch the molecules fly. A real-time Maxwell-Boltzmann curve plots the molecular velocity distribution.
*   **Reflection:** Students calculate how pressure emerges from millions of microscopic collisions against the container walls, resolving the relationship between mass, speed, and kinetic energy.
*   **Common Misconceptions:**
    *   *MISCONCEPTION_HEAVY_GAS_HAS_MORE_KE*: Believing heavier gas molecules have higher average kinetic energy at the same temperature (forgetting that $K_{avg} = 3/2 \cdot k_B \cdot T$, independent of mass).
    *   *MISCONCEPTION_COLD_IS_SLOW_STATIC*: Believing cooling a gas slows all molecules down to a uniform speed, rather than shifting the statistical speed distribution.
*   **Core Memory:** Seeing the light Helium spheres zip around like frantic fireflies while the massive Carbon Dioxide blocks lumber along slowly, yet knowing both carry the exact same average thermal punch.
*   **Emotional Arc:** Technical diagnosis of a gas leak $\rightarrow$ Awe of microscopic chaos $\rightarrow$ Stabilization and control.
*   **Scientific Discovery:** *Boltzmann's Statistical Dance*—discovering that temperature is not a uniform speed, but a collective statistical spread of microscopic kinetic energies.
*   **Teacher Notes:** Ideal for bridging the gap between macroscopic state variables ($P, V, T$) and microscopic atomic speeds.
*   **Estimated Duration:** 20 Minutes.
*   **Dependencies on previous Expeditions:** Expedition 03 & 08 (kinetic work and thermal systems).

### 10. Expedition Name: Harmonic Echoes (Oscillations & Waves)
*   **Core Scientific Concept:** Simple Harmonic Motion (SHM), restoring force, pendulum period, longitudinal & transverse waves, resonance, and the Doppler Effect.
*   **Story Premise:** To map the interior of a massive, hollow ice-sheet on Enceladus, you must construct a seismic sound array. By tuning a seismic pendulum to match the ice shelf's natural resonant frequency, you can bounce longitudinal waves deep into the core, recovering the hidden cavern profiles.
*   **World / Environment:** The Crystal Spires of Enceladus—majestic, towering ice sheets catching the golden glare of Saturn's distant ring system.
*   **Natural Interaction Language:** A massive suspended physical pendulum with real-time sinus wave chart drawing. Sliders adjust pendulum length ($L$), weight mass ($m$), and surface gravitational acceleration ($g$).
*   **Prediction:** *"Predict if a heavy pendulum bob oscillates faster or slower than a light bob of identical length when dropped on Enceladus."*
*   **Experiment:** Release the seismic pendulum. A pen traces a perfect, glowing sine-wave across a moving parchment scroll. If the length is tuned correctly, the ice spires hum in spectacular resonance.
*   **Reflection:** Calculate the restoring force equation ($F = -m \cdot g \cdot \sin\theta$) and prove the period of oscillation is purely dependent on length and gravity, not mass.
*   **Common Misconceptions:**
    *   *MISCONCEPTION_SHM_MASS_DEPENDENCE*: Believing a heavier pendulum bob oscillates slower (confusing spring harmonic periods with gravity pendulum periods).
    *   *MISCONCEPTION_WAVE_TRANSPORTS_MATTER*: Believing water or air waves carry the physical molecules forward across space, rather than just passing energy.
*   **Core Memory:** Tuning the pendulum length to the exact resonance threshold, watching the ice spires glow with internal turquoise light as the sine-wave trace aligns with the acoustic grid.
*   **Emotional Arc:** Silent, cold exploration $\rightarrow$ Rhythmic, harmonic tuning $\rightarrow$ Beautiful, resonant symphony of light and sound.
*   **Scientific Discovery:** *The Harmonic Signature*—discovering that nature's most diverse cycles, from guitar strings to orbits to seismology, are bound to the clean, cyclical math of a circle.
*   **Teacher Notes:** Provides a stunning visual synthesis of circular motion, trigonometry, and wave mechanics.
*   **Estimated Duration:** 30 Minutes.
*   **Dependencies on previous Expeditions:** Expedition 01 & 04 (unifies acceleration vectors and gravitational constants into cyclical harmonic oscillations).

## 🛠️ Implementation Strategy

The engine is already architected to ingest dynamic json configurations. To implement this roadmap:

1.  **Phase-Out Secondary Subjects:** De-prioritize the indexing of Chemistry, History, and Literature in the constellation views.
2.  **Modular Physics Content Expansion:** Author rich, schema-compliant JSON modules for each of the 10 expeditions inside `/src/content/physics_class_11/`.
3.  **UI Specializations:** Strengthen the `MissionActiveView` canvas engine so that different canvas renderers are activated based on the `coreInteraction` flag (e.g., `"PENDULUM_SHM"`, `"THERMODYNAMIC_PV"`, `"STRESS_STRAIN_TESTER"`).
4.  **Campaign Progress HUD:** Implement visual lock icons on the `ConstellationMap` tracking prerequisite pathways based on the `unlockConditions` metadata.
